DeskLink

Install this small app so your support team can see your screen and help you remotely.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 / 11 · about 80 MB · Free · direct installer (.exe)

Not available yet. Please ask your support team for the installer.

How to install

1
Click the button above and save the file. If your browser asks, choose Keep.
2
Open the downloaded ZIP, right-click → Extract All, then run DeskLink-Setup.exe. If you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway.
3
The app opens and asks for your installation code — type the 9-digit code your support team gave you.
4
That's it. Your computer shows as Available to your support team and stays that way until you click Disable Support Access in the app.
Download for Mac

macOS 11 or later · about 100 MB · Free · Intel Mac version Macs from 2020 onward are usually Apple Silicon (M-series). Older Macs need the Intel version.

The Mac version is coming soon. It is in final testing — your support team will let you know as soon as it is ready.

How to install

1
Click the button above, open the downloaded DeskLink.dmg and drag DeskLink into the Applications folder.
2
Open it from Applications. If macOS warns about the app, right-click it → Open → Open, or use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
3
Type the 9-digit installation code your support team gave you.
4
The app asks for two macOS permissions — Screen Recording and Accessibility. Click Grant for each, switch the app on in System Settings, then press Restart in the app.
5
Done. Your Mac shows as Available to your support team and stays that way until you click Disable Support Access in the app.

You stay in control. Your support team can only connect while the app shows Active. Click Disable Support Access at any time to stop being reachable, or Disconnect Session to end a live session instantly. A notice on your screen shows whenever someone is viewing.

The app keeps running in the background (system tray / menu bar) so your support team can help after a restart. To remove it completely, uninstall it like any other app.

Some antivirus tools flag remote-assistance apps because they can control the screen — that is exactly what this one is for, and only with your knowledge.
