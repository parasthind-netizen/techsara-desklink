Why DeskLink

Everything a support team needs, nothing it doesn't

Built for unattended help desks and managed fleets — reliable, cross-platform, and private by design.

Windows & macOS

One console for a mixed fleet. The same agent experience on Windows 10/11 and on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Low-latency screen share

Adaptive quality over WebRTC keeps the picture sharp and the pointer responsive, even on a slow link.

Full remote control

Pixel-accurate mouse and keyboard across multi-monitor and high-DPI setups, with clipboard sync both ways.

Private per customer

Every customer gets an isolated workspace. No customer can see, reach, or interact with another customer's machines.

Always on, self-healing

The agent comes back within seconds if it is closed, and returns on its own after a reboot — ideal for unattended desks.

Logins for your whole team

Give each support person their own login. Everyone on your team reaches the same machines — and nobody else's.

How it works

From request to remote session in minutes

No public sign-up. Access is granted by our team, so every customer starts in their own secured workspace.

1

Get your Customer ID

Email support@techsarasolutions.com. Our team creates your private dashboard and sends you a Customer ID and login.

2

Install the agent

Create an installation code in your dashboard, run DeskLink on the computer and enter the code. The machine comes online straight away.

3

Connect & support

Log in, pick a machine, and view or control its screen securely. It stays reachable for unattended support until access is turned off.

Visible by design

The person at the computer always stays in control

DeskLink is consent-based remote assistance. It never hides, and it can always be switched off or removed by the person using the machine.

Always visible

A tray or menu-bar icon is always present, and an on-screen notice shows who is viewing whenever a session is live.

One click to stop

“Disable Support Access” makes the computer unreachable, and “Disconnect Session” ends a live session instantly.

Removable like any app

Uninstall it whenever you like — an uninstaller is provided on both Windows and macOS.
