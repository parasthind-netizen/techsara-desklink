Why DeskLink

Everything a support team needs, nothing it doesn't

Built for unattended help desks and managed fleets — reliable, cross-platform, and private by design.

Windows & macOS

One agent, both platforms — Apple Silicon and Intel included. Your dashboard manages a mixed fleet without a second thought.

Low-latency screen share

Hardware-encoded H.264 over WebRTC with adaptive quality, so dragging a window feels instant even on a slow link.

Full remote control

Pixel-accurate mouse and keyboard across multi-monitor and high-DPI setups, with clipboard sync both ways.

Private per customer

Every customer gets an isolated dashboard. No customer can see, reach, or interact with another customer's machines — ever.

Always on, self-healing

A built-in watchdog brings the agent back within seconds if it's closed or killed, and it returns automatically after a reboot.

Push-button updates

Staged rollouts with a canary step and one-click rollback — update the whole fleet safely, per platform, from the console.
